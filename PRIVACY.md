# Privacy — حساب الدورة الشهرية / Period Calculator / Calculadora Menstrual

This early version is offline-first. Cycle dates, symptoms, and notes are stored in an Android Room database local to the device. There is no account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or network permission in this version. Android backup and device transfer of app data are disabled.

The user may delete all records from Settings. Uninstalling the application also deletes its local app data. Notifications, exporting, and ads are not present in this version.

Menstrual and fertility predictions are estimates only, are not a reliable birth-control method, and must not be used for diagnosis or medical decisions.

This document describes the repository's current implementation and is not a substitute for the published store privacy policy.
