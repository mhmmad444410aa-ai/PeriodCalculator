# Period Calculator

**App name:** Period Calculator

**Short description:** Track periods, symptoms and cycle patterns privately, offline.

## Long description

Period Calculator helps you keep a simple, private menstrual journal. Record period dates, bleeding intensity and everyday symptoms. Explore a monthly calendar, review cycle length trends and compare symptoms over time.

**Features:**
- A visual calendar for recorded periods and estimated date ranges.
- Cycle-length averages, variation indicators and data-quality notices.
- Daily tracking for cramps, headaches, bloating, fatigue, mood and sleep.
- Period timeline, summary charts, and PDF or CSV export.
- Optional generic reminders and device authentication where supported.
- Local-first storage, no account and no cloud synchronisation.
- Arabic, British English and Brazilian Portuguese interface options.

**Health notice:** Predictions are estimates, not medical advice. Calendar-based fertility dates cannot reliably prevent or confirm pregnancy. Speak to a qualified clinician about worrying symptoms.
